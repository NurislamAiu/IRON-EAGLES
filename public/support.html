ArcheoAI — Support

ArcheoAI is a free educational application dedicated to the archaeological heritage of Kazakhstan. Explore ancient artifacts, learn about Saka, Sarmatian, and other ancient cultures, and use AI-powered features to dive deeper into history.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?
Open the app, tap "Sign Up" on the welcome screen, and follow the registration steps using your email address.
How can I delete my account?
Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Confirm the deletion, and all your personal data will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.
Is ArcheoAI free?
Yes, ArcheoAI is completely free. There are no paid features, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.
How does the AI assistant work?
The AI assistant uses advanced language models to answer your questions about artifacts and ancient cultures. It is designed for educational purposes only and may occasionally produce inaccurate information.
How can I report inappropriate content?
Tap the "Report" icon next to any artifact or piece of content. Our team reviews all reports within 24 hours and takes appropriate action.
What languages does the app support?
ArcheoAI currently supports Russian, Kazakh, and English.
